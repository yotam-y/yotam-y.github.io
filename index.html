About

I build the decision systems that let companies move fast without getting burned, whether that means a fraud-detection engine, a client-facing analytics program, or an entire data function that didn't exist before I got there.

Over the past several years I've owned wire-fraud prevention strategy for one of the largest banks in the country, run product analytics for a first-party fraud risk engine used by enterprise clients, driven search-relevance analytics for a product used by 500M+ people, and, earliest in my career, built a solar company's entire data and forecasting function from zero. The common thread isn't one industry or one title: it's taking ambiguous, high-stakes problems and turning them into structured, cross-functional programs with measurable outcomes, working shoulder-to-shoulder with data science, engineering, compliance, and client-facing teams to get there.
